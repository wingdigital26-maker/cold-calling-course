# Metrics and Math: Know Your Funnel

Cold calling without tracking is guessing. With five numbers and basic division, you know exactly what is working and what to fix. This file defines the numbers, shows you how to compute your own funnel, and works one through completely.

## The five numbers that matter

Track these every calling session. A spreadsheet with five columns is enough.

1. **Dials.** Every time you press call. Voicemails, no answers, wrong numbers all count as dials.
2. **Connects.** A human answered, and it was someone at the business (owner, office manager, receptionist). Voicemail is not a connect.
3. **Conversations.** You got past the opener and actually delivered your reason for the call and value statement to a decision maker or someone close to one. A hangup at "hi" is a connect, not a conversation.
4. **Meetings booked.** A specific day and time on the calendar. "Call me next week" is not a booked meeting. "Send me some info" is definitely not.
5. **Meetings held (shows).** They actually showed up.

## The rates between them

Each rate is just the next stage divided by the previous one:

- **Connect rate** = connects / dials
- **Conversation rate** = conversations / connects
- **Booking rate** = meetings booked / conversations
- **Show rate** = meetings held / meetings booked

Typical rough ranges for cold outbound to small local businesses (these swing widely with list quality and skill; treat them as orientation, not targets):

- Connect rate: roughly 10% to 30%
- Conversation rate: roughly 20% to 40% of connects
- Booking rate: roughly 5% to 20% of conversations
- Show rate: roughly 50% to 80%

## A sample funnel, worked through

Say you dial for two weeks and log the following. We will apply mid-range rates and do every step of the arithmetic.

**Start: 500 dials.**

**Step 1: Connects.** Assume a 20% connect rate.
500 x 0.20 = **100 connects**

**Step 2: Conversations.** Assume 30% of connects become real conversations.
100 x 0.30 = **30 conversations**

**Step 3: Meetings booked.** Assume 20% of conversations end with a calendar time.
30 x 0.20 = **6 meetings booked**

**Step 4: Shows.** Assume a 50% show rate (typical for a beginner who is not yet confirming meetings well).
6 x 0.50 = **3 meetings held**

So this funnel is: 500 dials → 100 connects → 30 conversations → 6 booked → 3 held.

**Dials per held meeting:** 500 / 3 = 166.7, so about 167 dials for each meeting that actually happens.

**Sanity check the whole chain as one multiplication:**
0.20 x 0.30 x 0.20 x 0.50 = 0.006, meaning 0.6% of dials become held meetings.
500 x 0.006 = 3. Checks out.

## Turning meetings into money (why the math motivates)

Extend the chain with your close rate and deal size. Say you close 1 in 3 held meetings and a client is worth $1,000/month.

- 3 held meetings x (1/3) close rate = **1 new client**
- So in this example, 500 dials = 1 client = $1,000/month of recurring revenue.
- That means each dial was worth $1,000 / 500 = **$2 of monthly recurring revenue**, before you got any better at calling.

Those exact numbers are made up for the illustration. Yours will differ. But once you compute your own dial value, rejection stops feeling like failure and starts feeling like piecework: every dial, answered or not, earned you a couple of dollars on average.

## How to diagnose your funnel

Compare your rates to the rough ranges above. Whichever rate is furthest below range tells you what to fix. One weak stage at a time:

- **Connect rate low (under ~10%)?** It is a list problem, not a you problem. Bad numbers, calling at bad hours, too many main lines instead of direct/mobile numbers. Fix the list and call at better times (see file 04 for hours; early morning and late afternoon often catch owners off the job).
- **Conversation rate low?** Your opener is getting you hung up on. Rework the first ten seconds. Record yourself and listen; you will hear the telemarketer voice immediately.
- **Booking rate low?** People talk to you but do not book. Usually a weak or missing ask: you are ending calls with "I'll send some info" instead of proposing a specific time. Or your value statement is features instead of outcomes. Revisit stages 3 and 5 in file 02.
- **Show rate low (under ~50%)?** You are booking soft meetings. Send a calendar invite on the call, confirm by text the day before, and make sure the meeting has a concrete deliverable they want ("I'll show you the competitor breakdown"), not just "a chat."

One warning: do not diagnose on small samples. 20 dials tells you nothing; the difference between 2 connects and 6 connects at that volume is coin-flip noise. Get to at least 200 to 300 dials per script variation before concluding anything, and change one variable at a time.

## Your tracking template

Minimum viable tracker, one row per session:

| Date | Dials | Connects | Conversations | Booked | Held | Notes |
|------|-------|----------|---------------|--------|------|-------|
| Mon  | 60    | 13       | 4             | 1      |      | New opener, felt smoother |

Compute your four rates weekly, not daily. Weekly smooths out the noise; daily makes you crazy.
