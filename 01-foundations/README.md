# 01 - Foundations

Everything you need to understand before you make your first dial: how the psychology works, what a call is structurally made of, how to measure whether you are improving, and the rules that keep you out of trouble. No scripts to memorize yet, just the mental model. All numbers in these files are rough industry ranges, not promises. Read in order:

1. [01-mindset.md](01-mindset.md) - rejection math, detachment, tonality, real volume expectations
2. [02-how-a-cold-call-works.md](02-how-a-cold-call-works.md) - the five stages of a call and what good sounds like
3. [03-metrics-and-math.md](03-metrics-and-math.md) - your funnel, the rates, and a fully worked example
4. [04-legal-and-etiquette.md](04-legal-and-etiquette.md) - TCPA, Do-Not-Call, calling hours, recording consent, manners
