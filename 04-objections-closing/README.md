# Section 04: Objections and Closing

This is where cold calls are won or lost. By this point in the course you can open a call and hold a short conversation. This section covers what happens when they push back, how to turn interest into a booked meeting, and how to leave when the answer is genuinely no.

The through-line: stay calm, tell the truth, ask small. No pressure tactics, no scripts that make you sound like a robot, no wearing people down.

## Reading order

1. **[01-objection-framework.md](01-objection-framework.md)**: The four-step pattern behind every objection response: acknowledge, clarify, reframe, ask again. Read this first; everything else builds on it.
2. **[02-common-objections.md](02-common-objections.md)**: The 10 objections you'll hear on nearly every call day, each with what it really means, two word-for-word responses, and what not to say.
3. **[03-booking-the-meeting.md](03-booking-the-meeting.md)**: The close: assumptive time offers, keeping the ask to 15 minutes, calendar invites, killing no-shows, and what to do the moment they say yes.
4. **[04-when-to-walk-away.md](04-when-to-walk-away.md)**: Recognizing a real no, ending gracefully, leaving the door open, and why burning bridges is the most expensive mistake in a small market.

Practice tip: read 02 out loud, twice, before your next call block. Responses you've said with your mouth come out naturally under pressure. Responses you've only read do not.
