# Who We Sell To

We call local home-service businesses. The owner answers their own phone, makes their own decisions, and lives or dies by whether their phone rings with jobs.

## The trades

Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, junk removal, pool service, pest control, remodeling, general contracting. If they drive a truck to a house and fix or build something, they are in our lane.

## What a good prospect looks like

The best prospects have a gap between how good their work is and how good they look online. Signals you will see on the call sheet (Module 2 covers how we find these):

- **Few Google reviews** (roughly 0 to 15). They are invisible next to established competitors.
- **No website, or a bad one.** Facebook page only, a one-page template site, something that looks ten years old, or a site that is broken on a phone.
- **Established but stale.** Been in business for years, decent reviews, but the website has not been touched since it was made and they publish nothing.

## Why they need us specifically

The owner is on a roof or under a sink all day. They know they should "do something about the website" and have known it for years. They do not have time, they do not know who to trust, and the last marketing company that called them was selling junk leads. We do the whole thing for them and they own the result.

## Who NOT to pitch hard

- Franchises and big companies with a marketing department. The owner is not the decision maker and the call goes nowhere.
- Businesses that already look great online: strong modern site, hundreds of reviews, active blog. Be honest, tell them they look sharp, and move on.
- Anyone who says they are on the Do-Not-Call registry or asks not to be called again. Log it, honor it, done. Module 1 covers the rules.
