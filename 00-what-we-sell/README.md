# Module 0: What We Sell

Before you make a single dial, you need to understand what you are offering. Not how to build it, not how to run it. Jack will teach you the operations side directly. Your job on the phone is simpler: understand the offer well enough to explain it in one sentence, answer basic questions honestly, and book the meeting.

Read in this order:

1. [The services](01-the-services.md) - what Wing Digital actually does, in plain English
2. [Who we sell to](02-who-we-sell-to.md) - the businesses we call and why they need us
3. [Talking about it on a call](03-talking-about-it.md) - how much to say, what to never promise, and where your job ends

The one-liner to memorize: **We help local home-service businesses get found on Google and turn that attention into booked jobs.**
