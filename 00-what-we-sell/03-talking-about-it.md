# Talking About It on a Call

You now know what we sell. Here is the most important part: **you are not selling the service on the phone. You are selling a 15-minute conversation with Jack.**

## Where your job ends

Your call has exactly one goal: the prospect agrees to a short call and it goes on the calendar. That's a win. Everything else, the pricing, the audit of their site, the specifics of what they need, the close, happens on that call. Not yours to carry.

This should feel like a relief. You do not need to be an SEO expert. You need to know the one-liner, the four services at the "problem it solves" level, and how to book a meeting (Module 4).

## How much to say

As little as gets the meeting. A good call spends most of its time on THEIR situation (their reviews, their website, what you noticed) and almost none explaining our machinery. The moment you are lecturing about SEO, you have lost them.

If they ask a question you can answer in one sentence honestly, answer it. If it takes more than one sentence, that IS the meeting: "That's exactly what Jack walks through on the call. Fifteen minutes, he'll look at your actual site while you're on. Does Tuesday or Wednesday work?"

## The honesty rules

These are hard rules. Breaking them gets meetings that fall apart and a reputation that follows the company around town.

1. **Never quote a price.** Not a number, not a range, not "probably around". "It depends on what you need, that's what the call is for."
2. **Never promise rankings or lead counts.** Not "we'll get you to number one", not "you'll get 20 more calls a month". Say what we do, not what we guarantee.
3. **Never fake knowledge.** If you don't know, say "Good question, I'll make sure Jack covers that." Prospects trust people who admit the edge of what they know.
4. **Never trash the prospect's current setup beyond what you actually saw.** "Your site doesn't work great on my phone" is an observation. "Your whole web presence is garbage" is an insult and probably wrong.
5. **Never claim we talked before, that someone referred you, or that this is a callback, unless it is true.**

## Your 30-second version

Memorize this shape, then make it yours:

"We're a marketing company that works with [TRADE] businesses. Short version: when someone in [CITY] searches for what you do, we make sure you show up, look better than the other guys, and get the call. Website, Google, reviews, the whole picture, and we run it so you don't have to touch it. I'm not going to pretend I can tell you what you need over the phone. That's the 15 minutes with Jack."
