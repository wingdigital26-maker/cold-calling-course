# The Services

Wing Digital is a marketing agency for local home-service businesses. Roofers, HVAC, plumbers, electricians, landscaping, junk removal, pool service, pest control, general contractors. Here is what we do for them, in the plainest possible terms.

You do not need to know how any of this is built. You need to know what problem each thing solves, because the problem is what you talk about on the phone.

## 1. Websites

**What it is:** We build or rebuild the company's website. Full multi-page site: home, services, service-area pages, about, contact, FAQs.

**The problem it solves:** Most local trade businesses have a site that is old, slow, ugly on a phone, or missing entirely. When a homeowner gets two quotes and checks both companies online, the better website wins the trust before anyone picks up the phone.

**How to say it on a call:** "When someone in [CITY] looks you up before calling, what they find is deciding whether you get the job. We make sure what they find wins."

## 2. SEO and content

**What it is:** We make the business show up higher on Google. That means fixing the site's technical foundation, building pages for each service and each city they work in, and publishing regular blog posts so Google sees an active, authoritative site.

**The problem it solves:** When a homeowner searches "roof repair [CITY]", the companies on page one get the calls. Everyone else gets nothing. Most small trade businesses have never done anything deliberate about this.

**How to say it on a call:** "Right now when people search for a [TRADE] in [CITY], your competitors are showing up ahead of you. That's calls going to them that could be going to you."

## 3. Ongoing content engine

**What it is:** After the site is right, we keep publishing for them. Weekly blog posts, service pages for new cities, Google Business posts. The business does nothing; it just happens.

**The problem it solves:** SEO is not a one-time fix. A site that stops publishing slides back down. Owners do not have time to write anything, so we do it for them, every week, on autopilot.

**How to say it on a call:** "We don't just fix it once. Every week your site gets new content, so you keep climbing while your competitors stand still."

## 4. Reviews and reputation

**What it is:** We help them get more Google reviews from happy customers and keep their online reputation matching the quality of their actual work.

**The problem it solves:** A company with 8 reviews loses to a company with 200 reviews, even when the 8-review company does better work. Review count is the first thing a homeowner sees.

**How to say it on a call:** "You've got [THEIR REVIEW COUNT] reviews and [COMPETITOR] up the road has [COMPETITOR REVIEW COUNT]. Your work isn't the problem. Your visibility is."

## What we are NOT selling

Keep this straight, because credibility dies the moment you claim something we do not do:

- We are not a lead-selling service like Angi or HomeAdvisor. We build assets the business owns.
- We do not promise a specific ranking or a specific number of leads. Nobody honest can.
- We do not do TV, radio, or billboards.
- Anything a prospect asks about that is not on this page: "Good question, that's exactly the kind of thing Jack covers on the call."

## The shape of the deal

You do not quote prices. Ever. Pricing depends on what the business needs, and that gets figured out on the 15-minute call with Jack. If someone pushes hard on price, the honest answer is: "It depends on what your site and your Google presence need. That's literally what the 15 minutes is for. It might be less than you think."
