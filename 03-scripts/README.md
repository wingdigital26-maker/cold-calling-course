# 03 - Scripts

Everything in this folder is about what you actually say on the phone. Not theory. Words.

A script is not something you read like a robot. It is a map so you never freeze. You will sound scripted for your first 50 calls no matter what, and that is fine. By call 200 the words are yours.

## Reading order

1. **[01-openers.md](01-openers.md)** - How to survive the first 10 seconds. Start here, because if the opener fails, nothing else in this folder matters.
2. **[02-value-statements.md](02-value-statements.md)** - How to explain what you do in one sentence that a busy business owner actually cares about.
3. **[03-full-script-template.md](03-full-script-template.md)** - The complete call, hello to booked meeting, with branches for interested, hesitant, and busy prospects.
4. **[04-discovery-questions.md](04-discovery-questions.md)** - The questions that turn a pitch into a conversation.
5. **[05-voicemail-and-callbacks.md](05-voicemail-and-callbacks.md)** - What to do when nobody picks up, which is most of the time.

## How to use these

- Read all five files once, front to back.
- Pick ONE opener and ONE value statement. Write your version by hand, out loud, for your niche.
- Print the full script template, fill in the placeholders, and keep it in front of you on calls.
- After every 25 calls, change one thing that felt clunky. Not five things. One.

The scripts here use placeholders like [NAME], [COMPANY], and [CITY]. Replace them before you dial, not while the phone is ringing.
