# Section 5: Practice and Tracking

Scripts do not make you good at cold calling. Reps do. This section covers how to practice before you burn real leads, how to review your own calls, how to track your numbers, and a 30-day plan that takes you from zero to consistent daily call blocks.

## Reading order

1. [01-solo-drills.md](01-solo-drills.md) - Practice alone before you ever dial a real number.
2. [02-roleplay-with-ai.md](02-roleplay-with-ai.md) - Use any AI chatbot as a free roleplay partner.
3. [03-call-review.md](03-call-review.md) - Review your own recorded calls with a simple scorecard.
4. [04-tracking-and-kpis.md](04-tracking-and-kpis.md) - Track dials, connects, conversations, and meetings so decisions come from data.
5. [05-30-day-plan.md](05-30-day-plan.md) - The week-by-week plan that ties it all together.

## The short version

- Drill your script out loud until it sounds like you talking, not you reading.
- Roleplay with an AI so your first objection is not from a real prospect.
- Record and review your calls. You will hear things you cannot feel in the moment.
- Track four numbers every day. Change nothing until you have a real sample size.
- Follow the 30-day plan. Consistency beats talent here.
