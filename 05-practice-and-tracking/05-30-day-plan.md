# The 30-Day Plan: Zero to Consistent Daily Call Blocks

Thirty days from now you can be someone who dials every day without dread. Not a master. A consistent operator with real data and a script that has survived contact with reality. That is worth more than any amount of theory.

Rules for the whole month:

- The dial targets below are rough guidance for someone building this around another job or business. Scale up if you have full days available. Never scale down mid-week because you "don't feel it."
- A dial block is sacred: calendar it, phone on do-not-disturb for everything else, no email between calls.
- Track every day using the template in [04-tracking-and-kpis.md](04-tracking-and-kpis.md).
- Do not touch your script until the plan says you may.

## Week 1: Learn and drill (no real dials yet)

Goal: script internalized, first objections survived, list built.

| Day | Actions |
|-----|---------|
| 1 | Read sections 1 to 3 of this course. Write your script draft using the script templates. Read it aloud 5 times. |
| 2 | Solo drills: opener aloud 10x, record yourself, listen back, re-record. Start your objection flashcards. |
| 3 | Tonality day: smile drill, pacing drill, downward-inflection drill on the ask. 10 flashcards. Build your call list: 50 prospects minimum with names and numbers. |
| 4 | First AI roleplay: 2 rounds of the easy prospect. Ask for the scorecard both times. Fix the single biggest issue. |
| 5 | AI roleplay: 2 rounds of the tough prospect, 1 gatekeeper round. Scorecards. 10 flashcards. |
| 6 | Dress rehearsal: full call simulation start to finish, recorded. Compare to your day 2 recording. Grow your list to 100+ prospects. |
| 7 | Rest, or light review. Read [03-call-review.md](03-call-review.md) and [04-tracking-and-kpis.md](04-tracking-and-kpis.md) so you are ready to track from dial one. Set up your tracking sheet. |

## Week 2: First 50 dials

Goal: break the seal. Volume target is modest on purpose; the win this week is dialing at all, every day.

| Day | Actions |
|-----|---------|
| 8 | 5-minute warmup (see [01-solo-drills.md](01-solo-drills.md)). Make your first 10 dials. They will be rough. That is the point. Log everything. Review 1 call after. |
| 9 | Warmup. 10 dials. Review your worst call with the scorecard. One tough-prospect AI roleplay in the evening targeting whatever broke. |
| 10 | Warmup. 10 dials. Note which times of day people actually answered. |
| 11 | Warmup. 10 dials. Review 2 calls. Are you asking for the meeting on every conversation? Check the recordings, not your memory. |
| 12 | Warmup. 10 dials. That is 50 for the week. Do the weekly review questions. |
| 13-14 | No dials. 2 AI roleplays focused on your lowest scorecard row. Refill your list to 150+. Do NOT rewrite the script; 50 dials is not enough data. |

## Week 3: Volume and review

Goal: roughly double the volume, tighten the feedback loop. Rough guidance: 20 to 25 dials per day.

| Day | Actions |
|-----|---------|
| 15 | Warmup. 20 dials in one or two timed blocks. Review 2 calls (one bad, one decent). |
| 16 | Warmup. 20 dials, weighted toward the answer-times you found in week 2. Review 2 calls. |
| 17 | Warmup. 20 dials. Evening: 1 gatekeeper roleplay if gatekeepers are eating you, otherwise tough prospect. |
| 18 | Warmup. 20 dials. Review 2 calls. Update your flashcards with any new objection you have actually heard this week. |
| 19 | Warmup. 20 dials. You are now past 150 total dials. Weekly review: full rollup, all five questions. |
| 20-21 | Light day or rest. Compare your week 3 ratios to week 2. Pick ONE change for week 4 using the decision table in [04-tracking-and-kpis.md](04-tracking-and-kpis.md). Now, and only now, are you allowed to change the opener if the data says so. |

## Week 4: Refine and scale

Goal: run your one change against real volume, lock in the habit that outlives this plan.

| Day | Actions |
|-----|---------|
| 22 | Warmup. 25 dials with the one change (or no change, if the data said dial more). Track it identically. |
| 23 | Warmup. 25 dials. Review 2 calls specifically listening for the changed element. |
| 24 | Warmup. 25 dials. Mid-week check: is the change helping, hurting, or too early to tell? (Almost always: too early. Keep going.) |
| 25 | Warmup. 25 dials. Refill list to keep 100+ fresh prospects ahead of you at all times. |
| 26 | Warmup. 25 dials. Weekly review. Judge the change now, with a real sample behind it. Keep it or revert it. |
| 27 | Listen to your day 2 recording, then your best call from this week, back to back. That gap is yours. |
| 28-30 | Design your permanent routine and start it: a fixed daily call block (rough guidance: 60 to 90 minutes, 20 to 30 dials), 5-minute warmup before, 2 call reviews after, weekly rollup every Friday, one AI roleplay per week on your weakest area. Put it on the calendar as a recurring block. |

## After day 30

The plan ends. The routine does not. From here, everything is the same loop forever: dial, track, review, change one thing at a time, only with enough data. Meetings come from volume plus small compounding fixes. There is no other trick, which is exactly why most people never do it, and exactly why it works for the ones who do.
