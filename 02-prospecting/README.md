# 02 - Prospecting

Before you can make a good cold call, you need someone worth calling. This section covers how to build a list of real prospects for free, research them fast, run your day from a call sheet, and get past gatekeepers without lying.

Time spent here pays off on the phone. A decent list plus 2 minutes of research beats a great script dialed at random numbers.

## Reading order

1. [Building a List](01-building-a-list.md) - free sources, what fields to capture, targeting signals, list hygiene
2. [Pre-Call Research](02-pre-call-research.md) - the 2-minute routine that turns a name into a personalized opener
3. [The Call Sheet](03-call-sheet.md) - how to run your daily dials: columns, statuses, timeboxing, callbacks
4. [Gatekeepers](04-gatekeepers.md) - who they are, how to get past them politely, and what never to do

Read all four before your first call block. They are short on purpose.
